## 🚀 About Me

I'm the Founder & CEO of **Softlogix Technologies** and a Digital Marketing Analyst with **15+ years** in paid search and SEO, serving clients across the **USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE and Pakistan**. I started running PPC campaigns on Upwork in 2009 and have since managed Google Ads, Microsoft Ads and Meta Ads accounts end to end: account structure, conversion tracking, bid strategy and creative testing.

What sets my work apart is that I also build the sites the traffic lands on. I ship **Next.js / React / TypeScript** applications with multilingual routing, headless CMS integrations and server-side tracking, and I design **programmatic SEO** systems that generate hundreds of location and service pages from structured data. Websites, tracking and ad spend are one pipeline, and I own all of it. I use **AI-assisted development** (Claude Code) to move faster without lowering the bar.
